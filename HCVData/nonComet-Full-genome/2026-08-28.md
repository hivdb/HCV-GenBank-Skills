# NS5B blank `ClosestGenotype` notes — 2026-08-28

`NS5B_AllSeq_NonComet_Coverage.csv` contains 122,161 accessions. Of these,
63,668 have a blank `ClosestGenotype`.

A blank value does not mean that the accession is not HCV. It means that the
workflow did not find a qualifying NS5B genotype-reference BLAST hit. The
genotype-assignment step requires a matching NS5B hit with at least 200 aligned
nucleotides.

Many input sequences are fragments from other genes. For example, `MW205376`
is 635 nt long and has a genotype 4 NS5A assignment with full NS5A target
coverage (amino-acid positions 26–93), but has no usable NS3 or NS5B match.

Coverage mapping and genotype assignment are separate BLAST steps. Therefore,
a small number of accessions can show NS5B overlap while still having a blank
`ClosestGenotype`: the coverage hit did not satisfy the genotype-assignment
criteria.
